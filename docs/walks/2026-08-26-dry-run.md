## Harness lessons (what the dry rung is for)

- Adapter passed CLI args in a form goldfish.mjs rejects — fixed, pinned
  by test, caught on the first real spawn.
- The journey goal was being dropped — `--goal` flag added.
- Scroll-vs-par ruling (folded): scrolls are reading, not wandering.
- OPEN RULING: same-page anchor links ("Assign the unassigned" →
  #handout) — press or scroll-equivalent? Counted as presses: novice 3×
  par caps UX at 60, pro 2× caps at 80. Anchor-exempt: novice 2 (cap 80),
  pro 1 (clean). The exempt reading matches what the anchors are.
- Hand-crank context lines can contaminate: "You just pressed 'Assign the
  unassigned'" led the goldfish to narrate having pressed "Assign them".
  The runner passes no narrative; neutral phrasing only.
- dana-committee.mjs is BROKEN on staging (capability alarm, free): a
  closed call no longer renders the CFP form, so cfpSubmit after
  `cfpClosesAt: past` throws — and no primitive can close a call after the
  pile exists. The closed-call station is currently unbuildable through
  real flows. Fix: a settingsUpdate primitive against the real settings
  form, then reorder the recipe (submit, then close).
